\section*{Summary}
I'm an experimentalist who builds wet-lab methods that generate data quickly, accessibly, and reproducibly, and I use that data to check whether AI-designed molecules behave as predicted. I have moved across yeast genetics, mammalian cell biology, and RNA biochemistry, picking up each new system quickly and working closely with computational and Eterna design teams whose molecules I test. I ran robotic genome-wide screens and set up automated live-cell imaging on my own, and I build protocols that others can run: I invented FAST-MaP, wrote it up as an invited step-by-step protocol, and transferred it to AI@HHMI. Turning expert science into procedures that others can execute reliably is the work I most enjoy, and automated labs are where I want to take it next.
